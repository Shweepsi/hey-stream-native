Your IPTV subscription, simply.

Hey Stream plays your Xtream Codes subscription on Android TV, tablets and phones. Designed for the remote, fast on small boxes, with no account and no ads.

Download Source code

What it does

  • Live with previewCategories, channels and video side by side; what’s on now and next.
  • Movies and seriesResume where you left off, next episode plays automatically.
  • Favorites and recentsIn all three sections, with search by name.
  • Parental controlAdult categories detected and locked behind a code.
  • Offline firstThe catalog is kept on the device and updated once a day.
  • Your data stays with youNo account, no ads, no analytics.

Good to know

Hey Stream provides no content: it only plays the IPTV subscription you add, with the server address, username and password from your provider.

Current versions are test builds for Android 11 and later (including Google TV). Free software under the GNU GPL 3.0 license.
