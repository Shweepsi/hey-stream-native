A clean, fast TV player for Android TV, tablet and phone.

Hey Stream plays the sources you bring: live channels, movies and series, with the remote. No account, no ads.

Download Source code

Hey Stream is only a player. It does not provide, sell or recommend any channel, stream or subscription, and is not affiliated with any provider. You are responsible for having the right to watch what you add.

What it does

  • Live with previewCategories, channels and video side by side; what’s on now and next.
  • Movies and seriesResume where you left off, next episode plays automatically.
  • Favorites and recentsIn all three sections, with search by name.
  • Parental controlA code protects the categories you choose.
  • Offline firstThe catalog is kept on the device and updated once a day.
  • Your data stays with youNo account, no ads, no analytics.

Good to know

Compatible with the Xtream Codes API: you enter your source’s server address, username and password. Current versions are test builds for Android 11 and later (including Google TV). Free software under the GNU GPL 3.0 license.
